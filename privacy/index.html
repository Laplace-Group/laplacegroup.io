Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Laplace Group respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us or otherwise interact with us in connection with this website. Additional privacy notices may apply to individual products or services.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, telephone number, company details and the contents of your enquiries or correspondence. The information we collect depends on how you interact with us.

When you use our website, we and our service providers may collect technical and usage information, such as your IP address, browser and device type, referring pages, pages visited and the date and time of your visit.

How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • Manage our relationship with you and fulfil requests you make.
  • Operate, maintain and improve our website and understand how it is used.
  • Protect our website, systems and users, including detecting and preventing misuse.
  • Meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect our rights.

Where required by applicable law, we will obtain your consent before collecting, using or disclosing your personal information for a particular purpose.

Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies, analytics and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand visitor activity and improve the website. These technologies may collect information about your device and how you use our website.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect some website features. Where consent is required for particular technologies, we will request it before using them.

How we share information

We may share personal information with service providers that help us operate our website and business, including providers of technology, communications, analytics and professional services. Information is shared as needed for the relevant purpose and subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection arrangements.

We may also disclose information where required by law, in response to lawful requests from public authorities, or where necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others. We do not sell personal information.

International transfers

Personal information may be stored or processed in countries other than the country in which you are located. Where we transfer personal information internationally, we take steps to ensure it receives the protection required by applicable law.

Retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including any applicable legal, accounting or record-keeping requirements. When it is no longer needed, we take steps to delete it or make it anonymous.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information, object to or restrict certain uses, or withdraw consent where we rely on it. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that was lawful before the withdrawal.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and ask for information needed to process your request. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

Other websites

Our website may link to websites operated by other organisations. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to review.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact us

For questions about this policy, privacy concerns or requests about your personal information, contact Laplace Group at contact@laplacegroup.io.
